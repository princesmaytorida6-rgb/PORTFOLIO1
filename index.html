About Me 💗

A little story about who I am.

Hi! I'm Princess May Torida, a Grade 10 student of Mapurao Integrated Learning School (MILS) in Allacapan District.

I am currently serving as a Class President, where I continue to develop my leadership, communication, teamwork, responsibility, and service skills.

I am also part of the Allied Youth Leaders (AYL), an organization composed of student leaders from different schools around Allacapan.

Beyond academics and leadership, I am a Senior Scout of the Girl Scouts of the Philippines, a dancer, and a player. I believe that leadership is not only about having a position — it is about serving others, listening to people, and being willing to take action.

I am also a consistent honor student since elementary school, and I continue to challenge myself to grow academically, personally, and as a young leader.

My Leadership Vision 🌷

The leader I aspire to become.

Lead • Serve • Inspire ✨

I aspire to become the SSLG President of MILS and, in the future, the Municipal Federated Supreme Secondary Learner Government President of Allacapan for 2027.


My goal is to become a student leader who listens to learners, values their voices, promotes cooperation, and creates meaningful opportunities for students to participate and grow.


I believe that leadership is not measured only by a title. It is demonstrated through service, responsibility, integrity, teamwork, and the courage to make a positive difference.


"Leadership begins when we choose to serve." 💗
